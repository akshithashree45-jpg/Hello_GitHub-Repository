
# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a friendly, safe and welcoming environment for everyone.

## Expected Behaviour
- Be respectful to everyone.
- Use polite language.
- Help other contributors.
- Accept feedback positively.
- Respect different opinions.

## Unacceptable Behaviour
- Harassment or bullying.
- Discrimination.
- Offensive comments.
- Disrespectful communication.

## Reporting
Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainer through GitHub.

## Contact Information
For any concerns, contact the repository owner through GitHub.

## Our Commitment
We aim to maintain a respectful and inclusive environment for all contributors.
